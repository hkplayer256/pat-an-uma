## Character Requests

Requests are welcome, but please follow these rules so they're easy to handle.

1. **Search first.** Check open and closed issues so you don't post a
   duplicate.
2. **One character per issue.**
3. **Include the basics.** Give the character's name (English and
   Japanese if you know it).
4. **Don't send assets.** Don't upload or link models, textures, or other
   game files in issues, pull requests, or DMs. I source anything I use
   myself, and I won't accept assets from requests. Files taken from the
   game belong to Cygames, and they could get the project taken down.
5. **No pressure.** Don't ping or bump an issue to rush it. There's no
   timeline and no guarantee a request will be added.
6. **Keep it clean.** Requests that involve sexual or violent content
   will be closed, in line with Cygames' fan-content guidelines.
7. **I can say no.** Requests may be declined or closed without a
   detailed reason, and it's not personal.

## No Paid Requests

This is a free, non-commercial fan project. Characters, features, and
fixes are never added in exchange for money, donations, gifts, or any
other payment.

- Don't offer to pay the developer to add a specific character.
- Offers of payment won't be accepted, and they won't move a request
  up the list.
- Character requests are welcome as normal issues, but nothing is
  guaranteed.

Umamusume and its characters belong to Cygames, Inc. This is an
unofficial fan project with no affiliation to Cygames, and it follows
their fan-created content guidelines.
