## No Paid Requests

This is a free, non-commercial fan project. Characters, features, and
fixes are never added in exchange for money, donations, gifts, or any
other payment.

- Don't offer to pay the developer to add a specific character.
- Offers of payment won't be accepted, and they won't move a request
  up the list.
- Character requests are welcome as normal issues, but nothing is
  guaranteed.

Umamusume and its characters belong to Cygames, Inc. This is an
unofficial fan project with no affiliation to Cygames, and it follows
their fan-created content guidelines.
